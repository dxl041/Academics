# 译文_中英对照 — Connecting 100K+ GPUs: Building the Communication Stack for Large-Scale LLM Training

> SIGCOMM '26，Meta，页 505–518，CC BY 4.0。逐段 EN/CN 对照。

## 1 Introduction

EN: The rapid evolution of Large Language Models (LLMs) has transitioned from scaling single-node performance to architecting massive-scale distributed systems. Training state-of-the-art models now requires orchestrating over 100K GPUs in a single job, a scale that pushes existing datacenter networking and communication stack to its limit. At this magnitude, the conventional approaches to collective communication encounter scaling challenges:

CN: 大语言模型（LLM）的快速演进，重心已从单节点性能扩展转向大规模分布式系统的架构设计。训练最先进的模型如今需要在单个作业里调度超过 10 万块 GPU，这一规模把现有数据中心网络与通信栈逼到了极限。在这种量级下，传统的集合通信方案会遇到如下扩展挑战：

EN: Infrastructure Constraints: The physical topology required to connect GPUs spans multiple datacenter buildings. This introduces high bandwidth-delay product (BDP) links and variant round-trip times (RTT) where latency increases by up to 30× compared to intra-rack traffic. Such disparities cause standard transport protocols to struggle with maintaining throughput consistency across the fabric.

CN: 基础设施约束：连接 GPU 所需的物理拓扑横跨多栋数据中心楼，带来了高带宽延迟积（BDP）链路和差异巨大的往返时延（RTT）——延迟最高可比机架内流量高 30 倍。这种差异使标准传输协议难以在整个网络平面上维持一致的吞吐。

EN: New Workloads: The widespread adoption of Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures fundamentally alters traffic characteristics. Unlike dense models with predictable communication, MoE models rely on dynamic token routing that generates highly bursty, irregular all-to-all patterns. This creates massive transient congestion hotspots that challenge traditional network load balancing.

CN: 新型负载：混合专家（MoE）架构的广泛采用从根本上改变了流量特征。与通信模式可预测的稠密模型不同，MoE 模型依赖动态 token 路由，产生高度突发、不规则的 all-to-all 流量模式，造成大量瞬时拥塞热点，对传统网络负载均衡构成挑战。

EN: Overheads of "Lightweight" Operations: Operations traditionally considered lightweight begin to impose prohibitive overheads. Collective initialization relying on global barriers or O(N²) topology discovery can degrade job restart times to several minutes or even hours—a delay that is unsustainable given the cluster's Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF). Furthermore, standard libraries can consume roughly 10GB of HBM (12.5% of an 80GB H100), directly competing with model parameters for limited memory.

CN: "轻量"操作的开销：传统上被视为轻量的操作开始带来难以承受的开销。依赖全局屏障或 O(N²) 拓扑发现的集合通信初始化，会把作业重启时间拖到几分钟甚至几小时——考虑到集群的平均故障间隔（MTBF），这种延迟不可持续。此外，标准库会占用约 10GB 的 HBM（相当于 80GB H100 的 12.5%），直接与模型参数争夺有限内存。

EN: Operational Reliability: As the cluster scales, hardware failures shift from rare anomalies to daily occurrences. Ensuring high training "goodput" demands a suite of fault localization, performance monitoring, and large-scale emulation tools capable of functioning under extreme concurrency.

CN: 运维可靠性：随着集群规模扩大，硬件故障从偶发异常变成每天都会发生。要保证较高的训练"有效产出"（goodput），需要一整套故障定位、性能监控和大规模仿真工具，且能在极端并发下正常工作。

EN: To overcome these, we innovate at three categories:

CN: 为应对这些挑战，我们在三个层面进行创新：

EN: At the communication library layer, we present CCLX, designed to address the key overhead of initialization and memory consumption. We introduce scalable initialization via three key mechanisms: eager initialization, asynchronous I/O bootstrapping, and lazy initialization resource allocation. Our solution can reduce the initialization time by 11×. To tackle the memory constraints of CCLX to leave more memory to model training, we introduce lazy memory management which implements lazy channel provisioning, on-demand buffer allocation, and a slab allocator for metadata, which collectively over-provisioning and reduces communication memory overhead by 2×.

CN: 在通信库层面，我们提出 CCLX，用于解决初始化和内存消耗这两项关键开销。我们通过三个关键机制实现可扩展初始化：eager 初始化、异步 I/O 引导、懒初始化资源分配，可将初始化时间降低 11 倍。为缓解 CCLX 的内存约束、把更多内存留给模型训练，我们引入懒内存管理，实现懒通道供给、按需缓冲区分配和面向元数据的 slab 分配器，这些机制共同将通信内存开销降低 2 倍。（注：原文 "which collectively over-provisioning and reduces" 有语法瑕疵，按文意译）

EN: At the transport layer, while zero-copy collectives minimize control plane latency, they often cause excessive switch buffer build-up due to bursty, unsegmented transfers—a problem exacerbated by MoE workloads. We introduce Dynamic Queue Pair Load Balancing (DQPLB), a transport mechanism that combines the low latency of zero-copy with the flow control of segmentation. By dynamically tuning outstanding data limits and queue pair counts to match the BDP of specific paths (e.g., cross-datacenter vs. intra-zone), DQPLB reduces switch buffer occupancy by an order of magnitude while maintaining near-peak throughput.

CN: 在传输层，零拷贝（zero-copy）集合通信虽能最小化控制面延迟，但由于突发、不分段的传输，常常导致交换机缓冲区过度堆积——MoE 负载进一步加剧了这一问题。我们提出动态队列对负载均衡（DQPLB），一种同时结合零拷贝低延迟与分段流控的传输机制。通过动态调整在途数据上限和队列对数量以匹配特定路径的 BDP（如跨数据中心 vs. 区内），DQPLB 把交换机缓冲区占用降低一个数量级，同时保持接近峰值的吞吐。

EN: For the operational tooling, we have developed a comprehensive toolset comprising three key components in Section 5: an automated fault analyzer that distinguishes root-cause failures from cascading stalls using inter-collective dependency graphs, drastically reducing diagnosis time in environments where failures occur frequently; PerfProfiler, a lightweight instrumentation framework that correlates algorithmic and transport-level metrics to pinpoint bottlenecks; and a distributed CPU emulation environment that utilizes library interposition to validate control-plane scalability on CPU hardware, avoiding the cost of testing on GPUs.

CN: 在运维工具方面，我们开发了一整套工具集（详见第 5 节），含三个关键组件：自动化故障分析器，利用集合通信间依赖图把根因故障与级联停滞区分开，在故障频发的环境中大幅缩短诊断时间；PerfProfiler，一个轻量级插桩框架，将算法层与传输层指标相关联以定位瓶颈；以及一个分布式 CPU 仿真环境，利用库拦截在 CPU 硬件上验证控制面的可扩展性，避免在 GPU 上测试的高昂成本。

EN: Overall, our experience is that reaching 100K+ GPUs requires co-designing the collective library, transport mechanisms, and operational observability: at this scale, naive reuse of existing stacks fails under multi-building RTT/BDP regimes, MoE-induced burstiness, and frequent hardware faults. By rethinking initialization and HBM management in CCLX, introducing DQPLB for BDP-aware flow control, and building white-box tooling for fault localization and profiling, we make large-scale LLM training both performant and operationally robust.

CN: 总体而言，我们的经验是：要达到 10 万卡以上的规模，必须对通信库、传输机制和运维可观测性进行协同设计——在这一规模下，直接复用现有技术栈会在多楼栋 RTT/BDP 环境、MoE 引发的突发流量和频繁的硬件故障面前失效。通过重构 CCLX 的初始化与 HBM 管理、引入面向 BDP 感知流控的 DQPLB，以及构建用于故障定位与性能剖析的白盒工具，我们使大规模 LLM 训练既高性能又运维稳健。

## 2 Background（背景）

### 2.1 The 100K+ GPU Network（10 万+ GPU 网络）

EN: Meta's RoCE-based network has been evolving to meet the throughput and latency requirements of large-scale generative AI training. In its latest iteration, the network supports 100K+ GPUs; throughout this paper we refer to this deployment as the Cluster. Figure 1 summarizes the physical layout and the main hierarchy of the fabric. Table 2 illustrates the scale and latency of different components. We highlight three characteristics that shape our design:

CN: Meta 基于 RoCE 的网络一直在持续演进，以满足大规模生成式 AI 训练对吞吐和延迟的要求。在其最新一代中，该网络支持 10 万+ GPU；本文通篇将这一部署称为 Cluster（集群）。图 1 概括了该交换结构的物理布局和主要层级。表 2 展示了不同组件的规模与延迟。我们着重强调塑造我们设计的三个特征：

EN: Multi-building: The Cluster spans multiple datacenter (DC) buildings (Figure 1). Each building contains up to eight AI Zones [8]. Each AI Zone can support up to 288 racks with 16 GPUs per rack (∼4K GPUs per zone), yielding up to ∼32K GPUs per building. Scaling beyond a single building is therefore necessary to reach 100K+ GPUs, and it introduces longer links and greater variability in latency.

CN: Multi-building（多建筑）：Cluster 横跨多个数据中心（DC）建筑（图 1）。每栋建筑最多包含八个 AI Zone [8]。每个 AI Zone 最多可支持 288 个机架、每机架 16 个 GPU（每个 zone 约 4K GPU），从而每栋建筑最多可达约 32K GPU。因此，要达到 10 万+ GPU，就必须扩展到单栋建筑之外，而这会引入更长的链路和更大的延迟波动。

EN: Multi-hop fabric: The Cluster uses a multi-stage Clos topology that connects all GPUs via a single high-performance RoCE fabric. Within this fabric, the Rack Training Switch (RTSW) connects GPUs within a rack, Cluster Training Switches (CTSW) connect racks within an AI Zone, and Aggregator Training Switches (ATSW) connect CTSWs across a datacenter building; the ATSW layer is a fully connected mesh. This hierarchy provides incremental scale-out—additional buildings can be added over time—and, compared to the previous generation [8], reduces the cross-AI-Zone over-subscription ratio from 1:7 to 1:2.8 to better support multi-dimensional parallelism at larger scale.

CN: Multi-hop fabric（多跳交换结构）：Cluster 采用多级 Clos 拓扑，通过单一高性能 RoCE 交换结构连接所有 GPU。在该交换结构内部，Rack Training Switch（RTSW，机架训练交换机）连接同一机架内的 GPU，Cluster Training Switch（CTSW，集群训练交换机）连接同一 AI Zone 内的机架，Aggregator Training Switch（ATSW，汇聚训练交换机）连接数据中心建筑内的 CTSW；ATSW 层是一个全连接网状结构。这种层级提供了增量的横向扩展能力——可以随时间推移增加更多建筑——并且与上一代 [8] 相比，将跨 AI Zone 的过订阅比率从 1:7 降低到 1:2.8，以在更大规模上更好地支持多维并行。

EN: Latency: GPU-to-GPU latency grows quickly with hop count. GPUs within a rack have the lowest latency, while communication across racks within the same AI zone, across AI zones, and across datacenter buildings can experience 7×, 15×, and 30× higher latency, respectively. This increase comes from cumulative switching delays and longer cable runs. Note that the RTT increase is a direct result of larger cluster size, even within the same building, instead of a unique problem to our multi-building cluster. To achieve good collective performance in this hierarchy, the collective must inject enough data to fill the path bandwidth-delay product (BDP), but not so much that it creates persistent queueing and congestion.

CN: Latency（延迟）：GPU 到 GPU 的延迟随跳数增加而迅速增长。同一机架内的 GPU 延迟最低，而同一 AI Zone 内跨机架、跨 AI Zone 以及跨数据中心建筑的通信，延迟可能分别高出 7 倍、15 倍和 30 倍。这种增长来自累积的交换时延和更长的线缆走线。注意，RTT 的增长是更大集群规模的直接结果——即使在同一栋建筑内也是如此——而非我们这个多建筑集群所独有的问题。要在这种层级结构中获得良好的集合通信性能，集合通信操作必须注入足够的数据以填满路径的带宽延迟积（BDP），但又不能多到造成持续的排队和拥塞。

### 2.2 Large-scale LLM Workloads（大规模 LLM 工作负载）

EN: Recent developments in LLMs have significantly changed training workloads and introduced new networking requirements, particularly in the Cluster. We highlight three drivers and their impact on GPU communication: (1) Architecture: Recent LLMs increasingly adopt Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)[7], introducing irregular All-to-All collectives for token routing. (2) Context length: Context windows have expanded from thousands to millions of tokens, motivating Context Parallelism (CP) to support long-sequence training. (3) Multimodality: Modern LLMs are increasingly multimodal, introducing additional synchronization and data-handling complexity due to fused text and vision tokens.

CN: LLM 的近期发展显著改变了训练工作负载，并引入了新的网络需求，尤其是在 Cluster 中。我们着重强调三大驱动因素及其对 GPU 通信的影响：(1) Architecture（架构）：近期 LLM 越来越多地采用专家混合（Mixture-of-Experts，MoE）[7]，为 token 路由引入了不规则的 All-to-All 集合通信。(2) Context length（上下文长度）：上下文窗口已从数千 token 扩展到数百万 token，推动了用于支持长序列训练的上下文并行（Context Parallelism，CP）。(3) Multimodality（多模态）：现代 LLM 日益多模态，由于融合了文本与视觉 token，带来了额外的同步和数据处理复杂性。

EN: The immense scale of LLM training requires the use of various parallelization strategies to distribute the model and data across the Cluster. Parallelisms are fundamental techniques in large model training that break down a complex training job into smaller, concurrently executable tasks to manage memory, reduce computation time, and improve hardware utilization. To achieve this, we have employed a sophisticated combination of these methods, all of which rely on collective communication operations.

CN: LLM 训练的庞大规模要求采用各种并行化策略，将模型和数据分布到整个 Cluster 上。并行是大模型训练中的基础技术，它将复杂的训练任务分解为更小的、可并发执行的任务，以管理内存、缩短计算时间并提高硬件利用率。为此，我们采用了这些方法的精巧组合，而所有这些方法都依赖于集合通信操作。

EN: Hybrid Sharded Data Parallel (HSDP): Fully Sharded Data Parallel (FSDP)[37] is used together with Distributed Data Parallel (DDP) for sharding memory usage (e.g., optimizer states and gradients). FSDP uses AllGather for parameters and ReduceScatter for gradients, and DDP uses AllReduce.

CN: Hybrid Sharded Data Parallel（HSDP，混合分片数据并行）：Fully Sharded Data Parallel（FSDP，完全分片数据并行）[37] 与 Distributed Data Parallel（DDP，分布式数据并行）一起使用，用于分片内存使用（例如优化器状态和梯度）。FSDP 使用 AllGather 处理参数、使用 ReduceScatter 处理梯度，而 DDP 使用 AllReduce。

EN: Pipeline Parallelism (PP): Used to divide the model layers across GPU ranks. Point-to-point (P2P) communication is critical in PP. The implementation features zero-copy send/receive to remove interference between communication and computation, which in turn necessitates a Tensor Pool for P2P buffer management to improve buffer reusability.

CN: Pipeline Parallelism（PP，流水线并行）：用于将模型层划分到多个 GPU rank 上。点对点（P2P）通信在 PP 中至关重要。该实现采用零拷贝（zero-copy）发送/接收，以消除通信与计算之间的相互干扰，而这反过来又需要一个 Tensor Pool（张量池）来进行 P2P 缓冲区管理，以提高缓冲区复用性。

EN: Expert Parallelism (EP): Essential for training MoE models, EP shards the experts across a group of ranks to reduce memory pressure and mitigate the job from becoming limited by FSDP communication. Its primary communication primitive is the All-to-All collective, used to send tokens to the ranks where their assigned experts reside.

CN: Expert Parallelism（EP，专家并行）：对训练 MoE 模型至关重要，EP 将专家分片到一组 rank 上，以降低内存压力，并避免任务因 FSDP 通信而受限。其主要通信原语是 All-to-All 集合通信，用于将 token 发送到其分配专家所在的 rank。

EN: Context Parallelism (CP): A parallelism paradigm along the sequence length dimension, primarily supporting long sequence training by reducing memory consumption. CP Attention is implemented using an AllGather collective to exchange Key and Value tensors for the attention mechanism across the CP group ranks, while Query remains local.

CN: Context Parallelism（CP，上下文并行）：一种沿序列长度维度的并行范式，主要通过降低内存消耗来支持长序列训练。CP Attention 使用 AllGather 集合通信来实现，在 CP 组内的各 rank 之间交换注意力机制的 Key 和 Value 张量，而 Query 保持在本地。

EN: Tensor Parallelism & Sequence Parallelism: Used to shard individual layer weights and distribute computation. Collectives include AllReduce, AllGather and ReduceScatter.

CN: Tensor Parallelism（TP，张量并行）与 Sequence Parallelism（SP，序列并行）：用于分片单层权重并分布计算。所使用的集合通信包括 AllReduce、AllGather 和 ReduceScatter。

### 2.3 Discussion（讨论）

EN: While our overall infrastructure encompasses over 100K GPUs, the actual operational workloads shard this massive footprint into multi-dimensional parallelization domains. To correctly design and optimize the communication stack, it is critical to distinguish between the scaling requirements of different operations.

CN: 虽然我们的整体基础设施包含超过 10 万个 GPU，但实际的运维工作负载将这一庞大的规模切分为多维并行域。为了正确设计和优化通信栈，区分不同操作的扩展需求至关重要。

EN: The control plane operations, such as initialization, require true full-cluster participation. While the frequency of these operations is low, the scale makes them highly susceptible to serialization and network contention. As discussed in Section 3.2, such overhead becomes a major bottleneck at this scale impacting job restart times.

CN: 控制面（control plane）操作，例如初始化，需要真正的全集群参与。虽然这些操作的频率较低，但其规模使它们极易受到串行化和网络争用的影响。如第 3.2 节所讨论的，在此规模下，这类开销会成为影响任务重启时间的主要瓶颈。

EN: On the data plane front, hybrid parallelisms partition the cluster into much smaller, localized Process Groups. While the outermost parallelization schemes like HSDP can span thousands of GPUs, they primarily handle lower-frequency weight and gradient synchronization. The most communication-intensive parallelisms operate at an even tighter scale. For example, EP relies on demanding All-to-Allv operations for token routing; however, as shown in Figure 2c, this bursty traffic is structurally confined to small process groups typically consisting of less than 8 GPUs.

CN: 在数据面（data plane）方面，混合并行将集群划分为许多更小的、局部化的进程组（Process Group）。虽然像 HSDP 这样的最外层并行方案可以横跨数千个 GPU，但它们主要处理频率较低的权重和梯度同步。通信最密集的并行则运行在更紧凑的规模上。例如，EP 依赖要求苛刻的 All-to-Allv 操作用于 token 路由；然而，如图 2c 所示，这种突发性流量在结构上被限制在通常少于 8 个 GPU 的小进程组内。

EN: Next, we describe how CCLX optimizes library-level operations (Section 3), how DQPLB manages transport-layer congestion in high-BDP environments (Section 4), and how our operational tooling ensures system reliability (Section 5).

CN: 接下来，我们将描述 CCLX 如何优化库级操作（第 3 节）、DQPLB 如何在高 BDP 环境中管理传输层拥塞（第 4 节），以及我们的运维工具如何确保系统可靠性（第 5 节）。

## 3 GPU Communication Library（GPU 通信库）

### 3.1 CCLX

EN: The GPU communication library serves as the abstraction between training workloads and the NIC, interfacing through the verbs API layer. It translates collectives (e.g., AllReduce) into logical topology implementations (e.g., ring or tree) and further breaks those down into scheduling point-to-point data transactions between GPUs with verbs. These transactions require GPU-to-RDMA NIC support for optimal performance. The collective library schedules verbs calls over Queue Pairs (QP) created on NICs.

CN: GPU 通信库充当训练负载与 NIC 之间的抽象层，通过 verbs API 层进行对接。它将集合通信（如 AllReduce）转译为逻辑拓扑实现（如 ring 或 tree），并进一步将其拆解为通过 verbs 在 GPU 之间调度点对点数据事务。这些事务需要 GPU 到 RDMA NIC 的支持才能获得最优性能。集合通信库在 NIC 上创建的队列对（QP）之上调度 verbs 调用。

EN: While NCCL is used in the vast majority of the NVIDIA based training systems, we have found several challenges when leveraging it directly in our environment. To overcome these, we have developed CCLX¹. The key goal for CCLX is to provide a high-performance, scalable, and customizable communication framework for diverse application model needs and networking backends, while making it easier for communication developers to program.

CN: 尽管绝大多数基于 NVIDIA 的训练系统都使用 NCCL，但我们在自己的环境中直接使用它时发现了若干挑战。为克服这些挑战，我们开发了 CCLX¹。CCLX 的核心目标是为多样化的应用模型需求和网络后端提供一个高性能、可扩展且可定制的通信框架，同时让通信开发者更容易编程。（¹ 运行在 NVIDIA 平台上的 CCLX 版本也称为 NCCLX，运行在 AMD 平台上的版本称为 RCCLX。）

EN: Figure 3 gives an overview of the CCLX stack, which provides users a rich selection of communication semantics in three execution modes: Host-initiated APIs, Host-initiated APIs with GPU-resident metadata, and Device-initiated APIs. Each mode supports collective, point-to-point and remote memory access (RMA) semantics.

CN: 图 3 给出了 CCLX 栈的概览，它以三种执行模式为用户提供丰富的通信语义选择：主机发起的 API（Host-initiated APIs）、带 GPU 驻留元数据的主机发起 API（Host-initiated APIs with GPU-resident metadata）以及设备发起的 API（Device-initiated APIs）。每种模式都支持集合通信、点对点和远程内存访问（RMA）语义。

EN: To support the communication semantics, we have developed a custom transport framework CTran [31] that follows a host-driven communication framework with the design principle to promote zero-copy and stream multiprocessor (SM) free communication.

CN: 为支持这些通信语义，我们开发了一个定制的传输框架 CTran [31]，它遵循主机驱动的通信框架，其设计原则是促进零拷贝（zero-copy）以及不占用流多处理器（SM）的通信。

EN: When the model calls into the collective communication layer, CCLX dispatches the communication into either the baseline NCCL or the CTran code path. For custom communication operations (e.g., RMA, GPU-resident collectives) that do not have an implementation in baseline NCCL, they are directly dispatched to CTran. We allow users to explicitly choose the underlying baseline or CTran algorithms via environment variables.

CN: 当模型调用集合通信层时，CCLX 将通信分派到基线 NCCL 或 CTran 代码路径之一。对于基线 NCCL 中没有实现的定制通信操作（如 RMA、GPU 驻留集合通信），它们会直接分派给 CTran。我们允许用户通过环境变量显式选择底层的基线算法或 CTran 算法。

### 3.2 Scalable Initialization（可扩展初始化）

EN: Communication library initialization is an important overhead for large-scale training due to its high fault rates and frequent job restarts. For example, to ensure 90% effective training time on average, we can only afford 144 minutes of downtime per day. As we run jobs at 100K GPU scale, the expected failure count is around 49.15 times per day (assuming 4 restarts every 1000 servers per day), which means we can only afford 175 seconds per restart. Although existing technologies such as torchft [25] can reduce the blast radius of a single host failure, initialization overhead continues to worsen rapidly as training scales.

CN: 由于高故障率和频繁的作业重启，通信库初始化是大规模训练的一项重要开销。例如，为确保平均 90% 的有效训练时间，我们每天只能承受 144 分钟的停机时间。由于我们以 100K GPU 规模运行作业，预计每天故障次数约为 49.15 次（假设每 1000 台服务器每天重启 4 次），这意味着每次重启我们只能承受 175 秒。尽管诸如 torchft [25] 之类的现有技术可以缩小单台主机故障的影响范围，但随着训练规模的扩大，初始化开销仍在迅速恶化。

EN: When a training job restarts, communication is initialized by creating PyTorch process groups (PGs), where NCCL bootstraps the communication group (ncclCommInitRankConfig) that includes multiple sub-operations: each rank initializes local resources (CUDA runtime, topology detection, proxy servers), establishes control channels for peer discovery, and exchanges local state information with all ranks.

CN: 当训练作业重启时，通过创建 PyTorch 进程组（PG）来初始化通信，其中 NCCL 引导通信组（ncclCommInitRankConfig），该过程包含多个子操作：每个 rank 初始化本地资源（CUDA runtime、拓扑检测、代理服务器）、建立用于对等节点发现的控制通道，并与所有 rank 交换本地状态信息。

EN: Communication initialization time scales non-linearly due to serialized operations, network contention, and computational complexity. At 100K scale, initialization using NCCL requires more than 4 min, which goes beyond the tolerable restart time for reacting to failures. There are three key ideas to enable scalable initialization:

CN: 由于串行化操作、网络争用和计算复杂度，通信初始化时间呈非线性增长。在 100K 规模下，使用 NCCL 进行初始化需要超过 4 分钟，这超出了对故障做出反应所能容忍的重启时间。实现可扩展初始化有三个关键思路：

EN: Eager initialization: NCCL initialization ("Lazy Mode") requires each rank in a PG to independently create its communicator upon the first collective operation. This process involves the root rank broadcasting a unique ID (ncclUniqueId) via a centralized key-value store, followed by each rank calling ncclCommInitRankConfig to initialize its communicator. While this approach works for small-scale deployments, it suffers from two major drawbacks at scale: (1) excessive load on the key-value store due to repeated, independent creation for numerous PGs (e.g., 10+ PGs in a large model), and (2) inefficiency in creating sub-PGs in existing PGs because of the lack of state reuse and the need for redundant state exchange for every new group.

CN: Eager 初始化：NCCL 初始化（"Lazy Mode"）要求 PG 中的每个 rank 在首次集合通信操作时独立创建自己的 communicator。这一过程包括根 rank 通过集中式键值存储广播一个唯一 ID（ncclUniqueId），随后每个 rank 调用 ncclCommInitRankConfig 来初始化其 communicator。虽然这种方法适用于小规模部署，但在大规模下存在两大缺点：(1) 由于大量 PG（例如大模型中有 10+ 个 PG）的重复、独立创建，导致键值存储负载过重；(2) 由于缺乏状态复用，以及每个新组都需要冗余的状态交换，导致在现有 PG 中创建子 PG 效率低下。

EN: To resolve these scalability issues, CCLX introduces the Eager mode. It begins with each rank establishes a single global communicator that encompasses all global ranks (the default-PG). Subsequently, any sub-PG is derived or split from this pre-existing global communicator. By reusing the global communicator's established state, the need for further ncclUniqueId exchanges is eliminated. This fundamental change significantly reduces the cost of sub-PG creation and lowers the overall initialization overhead.

CN: 为解决这些可扩展性问题，CCLX 引入了 Eager 模式。它首先让每个 rank 建立一个涵盖所有全局 rank 的单一全局 communicator（即 default-PG）。随后，任何子 PG 都从这个预先存在的全局 communicator 派生或拆分而来。通过复用全局 communicator 已建立的状态，无需再进行 ncclUniqueId 交换。这一根本性改变显著降低了子 PG 的创建成本，并降低了整体初始化开销。

EN: Figure 4: Comparison between NCCL 2.21.5 and CCLX on default process group initialization.

CN: 图 4：NCCL 2.21.5 与 CCLX 在默认进程组初始化上的对比。

EN: Asynchronous I/O bootstrapping: NCCL bootstraps communicator by initializing a bootstrap server on rank 0. Each rank then communicates with this server to obtain the IP address and port of its subsequent rank, thereby forming a ring. To mitigate the risk of overloading the bootstrap server at larger scales (exceeding 128 ranks), each rank is required to stagger its connection request by waiting for a duration proportional to its rank ID, measured in milliseconds. At 100K scale, the final rank must wait for around 100 seconds to establish a connection with the bootstrap server. In practice, this will allow us to finish the bootstrap within the budget.

CN: 异步 I/O 引导（Asynchronous I/O bootstrapping）：NCCL 通过在 rank 0 上初始化一个引导服务器来引导 communicator。随后每个 rank 与该服务器通信，以获取其后续 rank 的 IP 地址和端口，从而形成一个 ring。为降低在更大规模（超过 128 个 rank）下引导服务器过载的风险，要求每个 rank 通过等待一段与其 rank ID 成比例的时长（以毫秒计）来错开其连接请求。在 100K 规模下，最后一个 rank 必须等待约 100 秒才能与引导服务器建立连接。在实践中，这将使我们能够在预算内完成引导。

EN: Lazy initialization resource allocation: The NCCL baseline allocates all resources during ncclCommInitRank, which can block the applications. CCLX defers resource allocation from initialization time to the first collective call, performing minimal necessary setup. When the first collective is invoked, algorithm specific resource allocation happens asynchronously in the background, overlapping with ongoing application work such as model loading, data fetching, and kernel computations. This approach hides allocation overhead within existing computation, further reducing time-to-first-collective in real-world training scenarios.

CN: 懒初始化资源分配（Lazy initialization resource allocation）：NCCL 基线在 ncclCommInitRank 期间分配所有资源，这可能会阻塞应用程序。CCLX 将资源分配从初始化时刻推迟到首次集合通信调用，只执行最小必要的设置。当首次集合通信被调用时，算法特定的资源分配在后台异步进行，与正在进行的应用工作（如模型加载、数据获取和内核计算）重叠。这种方法将分配开销隐藏在现有计算中，进一步缩短了真实训练场景中的首次集合通信时间。

EN: In addition to the architecture changes, we did a few optimizations that further improve the performance. For example, AllGather is used in bootstrap phase to exchange control data among participating ranks within a communicator. We optimized AllGather with bi-directional AllGather [12] (reducing steps from N−1 to N/2) and also evaluated tree-based algorithms (O(logN) vs O(N) scaling). We combined seven AllGather operations of gathering several data types to four AllGather operations to further reduce the overhead during initialization. We improved topology computation from O(N²) to O(N) complexity [11], consuming 10s at 48K ranks and around 100s at 100K scale. Additional improvements include eliminating unique ID broadcasts and optimizing P2P communicator creation for pipeline parallelism.

CN: 除架构上的改动外，我们还做了一些进一步改善性能的优化。例如，AllGather 在引导阶段用于在 communicator 内的参与 rank 之间交换控制数据。我们用双向 AllGather [12] 优化了 AllGather（将步骤数从 N−1 减少到 N/2），并评估了基于 tree 的算法（O(logN) 与 O(N) 的扩展对比）。我们将汇聚若干数据类型的七次 AllGather 操作合并为四次 AllGather 操作，以进一步降低初始化期间的开销。我们将拓扑计算从 O(N²) 复杂度改进到 O(N) 复杂度 [11]，在 48K rank 下耗时 10 秒，在 100K 规模下约 100 秒。其他改进包括消除唯一 ID 广播，以及为流水线并行优化 P2P communicator 的创建。

EN: Figure 4 shows the initialization performance results, comparing the baseline NCCL with CCLX. This represents an up to 11× improvement at 96K GPUs scale.

CN: 图 4 展示了初始化性能结果，对比了基线 NCCL 与 CCLX。这代表了在 96K GPU 规模下最高达 11 倍的改进。

### 3.3 HBM Management（高带宽内存管理）

EN: Resource utilization, including SMs, memory, and network resources, is an important factor in steady-state training. Overconsumption of these resources degrades training throughput and efficiency and, in extreme cases, can cause training job failures. In LLM training, this risk is amplified because each parallelism domain may instantiate its own communicators, causing cumulative resource consumption to grow with the number of communicators.

CN: 资源利用率（包括 SM、内存和网络资源）是稳态训练中的一个重要因素。这些资源的过度消耗会降低训练吞吐和效率，在极端情况下还可能导致训练作业失败。在 LLM 训练中，这种风险被放大，因为每个并行域都可能实例化自己的 communicator，导致累计资源消耗随 communicator 数量增长。

EN: Among these constrained resources, GPU high-bandwidth memory (HBM) is often the dominant bottleneck for LLM pre-training. When more HBM is available to the training job, it improves both throughput and eventual model quality by enabling larger batch sizes and supporting exploration and tuning over a wider range of hyperparameter configurations. However, in practice, pre-training runs frequently fail with out-of-memory (OOM) errors because the usable HBM is significantly below the device's specification. For example, on H100 GPUs, only about 60 GB of the advertised 80 GB HBM may be available after accounting for system overheads and framework reservations.

CN: 在这些受限资源中，GPU 高带宽内存（HBM，high-bandwidth memory）通常是 LLM 预训练的主要瓶颈。当训练作业可获得更多 HBM 时，它通过支持更大的 batch size 并支持在更广泛的超参数配置范围内探索和调优，来同时改善吞吐和最终模型质量。然而，在实践中，预训练运行经常因内存不足（OOM）错误而失败，因为可用 HBM 显著低于设备规格。例如，在 H100 GPU 上，扣除系统开销和框架预留后，标称 80 GB 的 HBM 可能只有约 60 GB 可用。

EN: Communication libraries are typically optimized for peak collective performance rather than resource efficiency. NCCL allocates substantial internal GPU buffers that are opaque to, and not shared with, the application in order to enable highly tuned pipelines across diverse hardware topologies and collective patterns. In today's multi-dimensional LLM training, which instantiates multiple parallelism groups and thus multiple NCCL communicators, these internal buffers are allocated separately for each communicator, leading to substantial HBM overhead. In LLM pre-training experiments, we observed NCCL consuming roughly 10 GB of HBM (≈12.5% of an 80 GB H100) across more than 10 parallelism groups, where each group is associated with a distinct NCCL communicator and maintains its own dedicated buffer allocations.

CN: 通信库通常针对峰值集合通信性能而非资源效率进行优化。NCCL 分配了大量内部 GPU 缓冲区，这些缓冲区对应用不透明且不与应用共享，以便在多样化的硬件拓扑和集合通信模式下实现高度调优的流水线。在当今的多维 LLM 训练中，由于实例化了多个并行组，从而实例化了多个 NCCL communicator，这些内部缓冲区为每个 communicator 单独分配，导致巨大的 HBM 开销。在 LLM 预训练实验中，我们观察到 NCCL 在超过 10 个并行组上消耗了约 10 GB 的 HBM（约占 80 GB H100 的 12.5%），其中每个组关联一个不同的 NCCL communicator，并维护其自己专用的缓冲区分配。

EN: We have found three main design choices in NCCL that lead to inefficient resource utilization.

CN: 我们发现了 NCCL 中导致资源利用率低下的三个主要设计选择。

EN: Eager communicator allocation: NCCL sustains high performance for copy-based communication by eagerly allocating substantial memory and peer connections at communicator initialization. Specifically, for each communicator, it allocates internal buffers and QPs per send/receive peer and per protocol (LL/LL128/SIMPLE). As the number of communicators grows, this strategy inflates memory usage—especially in workloads dominated by All-to-All communication, a pattern commonly observed in MoE training. NCCL further allocates algorithm-specific resources (e.g., ring) at runtime, even though training typically exercises only a subset of these paths, leaving the remainder underutilized. Additionally, communicators whose peers reside within an NVLink domain tend to receive even larger allocations, since NCCL can exploit additional buffering and concurrency to saturate NVLink's high bandwidth. However, because NCCL lacks knowledge of the actual communication pattern at initialization, this eager provisioning often results in substantial resource waste. Parallelism groups execute a fixed set of collectives over well-defined message-size regimes determined by the model configuration and scale. For example, a FSDP group typically performs AllGather and ReduceScatter using only the ring algorithm, making pre-allocation for tree-specific resources unnecessary.

CN: Eager communicator 分配：NCCL 通过在 communicator 初始化时急切（eager）分配大量内存和对等连接，来维持基于拷贝的通信的高性能。具体而言，对于每个 communicator，它为每个发送/接收对等节点以及每种协议（LL/LL128/SIMPLE）分配内部缓冲区和 QP。随着 communicator 数量增长，这种策略会膨胀内存使用——尤其是在以 All-to-All 通信为主的工作负载中（这是 MoE 训练中常见的模式）。NCCL 还会在运行时分配算法特定的资源（如 ring），尽管训练通常只用到这些路径的一个子集，其余部分则处于利用不足的状态。此外，对等节点位于 NVLink 域内的 communicator 往往会获得更大的分配，因为 NCCL 可以利用额外的缓冲和并发来饱和 NVLink 的高带宽。然而，由于 NCCL 在初始化时并不了解实际的通信模式，这种急切预留往往导致大量的资源浪费。并行组在由模型配置和规模决定的、明确定义的消息大小区间上执行一组固定的集合通信操作。例如，FSDP 组通常仅使用 ring 算法执行 AllGather 和 ReduceScatter，因此为 tree 特定资源进行预分配是不必要的。

EN: Channel over-provisioning: NCCL employs a multi-channel design to increase concurrency in data movement and improve network utilization. To maximize performance, each communicator provisions the maximum number of channels at initialization, and each channel maintains its own dedicated resources. This strategy becomes inefficient when a communicator does not require many channels to achieve near-peak throughput—for example, when it primarily executes small-message collectives—since a substantial fraction of per-channel resources remains rarely used.

CN: 通道过度预留（Channel over-provisioning）：NCCL 采用多通道设计来提高数据移动的并发性并改善网络利用率。为最大化性能，每个 communicator 在初始化时预留最大数量的通道，且每个通道维护其自己专用的资源。当某个 communicator 不需要很多通道即可达到接近峰值的吞吐时——例如当它主要执行小消息集合通信时——这种策略就变得低效，因为每通道资源中有相当大一部分几乎不被使用。

CN: 图 5：懒资源分配与懒通道预留示例。CCLX 通过只分配必要的通道和算法缓冲区来节省内存。

CN: 表 3：所提特性在 64K GPU 上 LLM 预训练中节省内存的示例。对于每个节省内存的特性，我们使用可比的配置分别进行了实验。

EN: Channel metadata alignment: NCCL's "channel" state includes metadata such as FIFO status and peer-connection information. NCCL places this metadata in HBM primarily to enable low-latency access by its kernels. Although the channel state is lightweight, it consumes around 2 MiB per channel due to alignment constraints imposed by CUDA low-level virtual memory management interfaces (e.g., cuMem APIs). This alignment and per-channel footprint increase HBM pressure and exacerbate memory fragmentation and waste. This effect is amplified at large scale–the total metadata footprint scales with the number of ranks in a communicator, accumulating to over 1GB at 100k scale.

CN: 通道元数据对齐（Channel metadata alignment）：NCCL 的 "channel" 状态包含诸如 FIFO 状态和对等连接信息之类的元数据。NCCL 将这些元数据放在 HBM 中，主要是为了让其内核能够低延迟地访问它们。尽管通道状态很轻量，但由于 CUDA 底层虚拟内存管理接口（如 cuMem API）施加的对齐约束，每个通道要消耗约 2 MiB。这种对齐和每通道的占用增加了 HBM 压力，加剧了内存碎片化和浪费。这种效应在大规模下被放大——总元数据占用随 communicator 中的 rank 数量而增长，在 100K 规模下累计超过 1 GB。

EN: To address these limitations, we have improved resource efficiency by introducing three corresponding mechanisms.

CN: 为解决这些局限，我们通过引入三个相应的机制来提高资源效率。

EN: Lazy communicator resource allocation: To mitigate the resource waste caused by NCCL's eager provisioning, we adopt a lazy strategy: it allocates algorithm-specific resources and CTran peer connection resources only when an algorithm is actually exercised at runtime. As the example illustrated in Figure 5, if a communicator only performs Tree algorithm for small AllReduce at runtime, we can only allocate Tree buffers on 1 channel, compared to NCCL would allocate resources in all channels and only use it partially. In an empirical study of LLM pre-training runs, enabling lazy connect alone reduced memory consumption by approximately 2.2GB as shown in Table 3.

CN: 懒 communicator 资源分配（Lazy communicator resource allocation）：为缓解 NCCL 急切预留造成的资源浪费，我们采用懒策略：仅当算法在运行时被实际使用时才分配算法特定资源和 CTran 对等连接资源。如图 5 中的示例所示，如果某个 communicator 在运行时只对小规模 AllReduce 执行 Tree 算法，我们可以只在 1 个通道上分配 Tree 缓冲区，相比之下 NCCL 会在所有通道中分配资源且只部分使用。在对 LLM 预训练运行的实证研究中，仅启用懒连接就减少了约 2.2 GB 的内存消耗，如表 3 所示。

EN: Lazy channel provisioning: To reduce resource waste due to NCCL channel over-provisioning, we allocate only the minimum number of channels initially and expands the channel count at runtime as needed. The key observation is that some communicators execute only small collectives over the lifetime of a training job and therefore do not require multi-channel concurrency to approach peak bandwidth. This mechanism complements lazy resource allocation, which defers algorithm-specific resource allocation across all channels. Lazy channel provisioning further avoids pre-allocation resources for unnecessary channels altogether. As illustrated in Figure 5, if a collective requires only 4 channels for a ring-based algorithm, CCLX refrains from allocating resources for the remaining 12 channels (assuming a 16-channel default). As shown in Table 3, this feature yields an additional ∼1.2 GB memory reduction.

CN: 懒通道预留（Lazy channel provisioning）：为减少 NCCL 通道过度预留造成的资源浪费，我们最初只分配最小数量的通道，并在运行时按需扩展通道数量。关键观察是，某些 communicator 在训练作业的整个生命周期内只执行小规模集合通信，因此不需要多通道并发即可接近峰值带宽。这一机制与懒资源分配互补，后者推迟了所有通道上算法特定资源的分配。懒通道预留进一步彻底避免了为不必要的通道预分配资源。如图 5 所示，如果某个集合通信操作对基于 ring 的算法只需 4 个通道，CCLX 就避免为其余 12 个通道分配资源（假设默认 16 通道）。如表 3 所示，这一特性额外带来了约 1.2 GB 的内存减少。

EN: Slab allocator for channel metadata: At large scale, per-channel metadata allocations becomes highly fragmented, leading to substantial memory waste. To mitigate this overhead, we implement a slab allocator that packs metadata from multiple communicators into contiguous GPU pages. By co-locating many channels' metadata within the same page, the allocator reduces both internal fragmentation and the number of pages that must be reserved. As shown in the example of Figure 6, a 2 MB GPU page can store metadata for roughly 3000 peers; without slab allocation, fragmentation forces the same metadata to be spread across multiple pages. All slab slots are released when a communicator is destroyed at the end of the training job, preventing resource leaks. As shown in Table 3, this optimization reduces memory usage by approximately 400 MB.

CN: 用于通道元数据的 slab 分配器（Slab allocator for channel metadata）：在大规模下，每通道元数据的分配变得高度碎片化，导致大量内存浪费。为缓解这一开销，我们实现了一个 slab 分配器，将来自多个 communicator 的元数据打包到连续的 GPU 页中。通过将许多通道的元数据共同放置在同一个页内，该分配器减少了内部碎片化以及必须保留的页数。如图 6 的示例所示，一个 2 MB 的 GPU 页可以存储约 3000 个对等节点的元数据；而如果没有 slab 分配，碎片化会迫使相同的元数据散布在多个页上。当 communicator 在训练作业结束时被销毁时，所有 slab 槽位都会被释放，从而防止资源泄漏。如表 3 所示，这一优化减少了约 400 MB 的内存使用。

EN: With these optimizations, we have reduced GPU memory usage by nearly 2× across more than 10 communicators at large scale, both in our evaluation (Table 3) and in production runs. These features reduce the number of RDMA QPs to below 2000 per NIC, thanks to the lazy QP allocation, which is within the capacity of NICs without performance degradation.

CN: 借助这些优化，在大规模下超过 10 个 communicator 上，我们将 GPU 内存使用降低了近 2 倍，这一点在评估（表 3）和生产运行中均已体现。得益于懒 QP 分配，这些特性将每个 NIC 上的 RDMA QP 数量降低到 2000 以下，处于 NIC 的容量范围内而不会造成性能下降。

## 4 Transport Layer（传输层）

### 4.1 Challenges（挑战）

EN: When the number of network hops increases, the GPU-to-GPU communication latency increases significantly. Specifically, GPUs within the same rack have the lowest latency, while communication across different racks within the same AI zone, across AI zones, and across separate datacenter buildings experience 7×, 15×, and 30× higher latency, respectively [31]. This increased latency is primarily due to cumulative switching delays and increased cable lengths.

CN: 当网络跳数增加时，GPU 到 GPU 的通信延迟显著上升。具体而言，同一机架内的 GPU 延迟最低，而在同一 AI 区域内跨机架、跨 AI 区域以及跨独立数据中心楼宇的通信，其延迟分别高出 7 倍、15 倍和 30 倍 [31]。这种增加的延迟主要源于累积的交换时延和更长的线缆长度。

EN: As we move to MoE models, the prevalent communication patterns can have an on-off pattern particularly with small messages. This traffic is naturally bursty [9], which results in higher buffer utilization, as we describe later in this section, and can result in increased latency, and fairness issues when mixed RTT flows compete for bandwidth and buffer.

CN: 随着我们转向 MoE 模型，主流的通信模式可能呈现开关式（on-off）特征，尤其是在小消息场景下。这种流量天然具有突发性 [9]，导致更高的缓冲区占用率（正如我们在本节稍后所述），并可能带来延迟升高，以及在混合 RTT 流竞争带宽和缓冲区时出现公平性问题。

EN: Managing latency is a tradeoff. The two-stage copy mechanism within NCCL requires clear-to-send control messages from the receiver to the sender on the critical path. To mitigate the high control-message latency of the two-stage copy solution, we implemented zero-copy collectives, enabling the NIC to perform RDMA directly. As a result, GPU-to-GPU exchanges require only a single control message from receiver to sender, significantly reducing network latency. However, zero-copy results in the entire message being handed off to the NIC, which results in bursty sends.

CN: 延迟管理是一种权衡。NCCL 中的两阶段拷贝机制要求在关键路径上由接收方向发送方发送 clear-to-send 控制消息。为缓解两阶段拷贝方案的高控制消息延迟，我们实现了零拷贝集合通信，使 NIC 能够直接执行 RDMA。结果是，GPU 到 GPU 的交换只需一条从接收方到发送方的控制消息，显著降低了网络延迟。然而，零拷贝会导致整条消息被直接交给 NIC，从而产生突发式发送。

EN: Figure 6: Slab allocator illustration: multiple metadata can fit into 2MB slabs to avoid waste.

CN: 图 6：slab 分配器示意图：多个元数据可以装入 2MB 的 slab 中以避免浪费。

EN: The increased burstiness impacts our congestion control strategy. We do not employ traditional congestion control mechanisms such as DCQCN to limit switch buffer occupancy due to the difficulty of tuning it correctly in a diverse environment [8]. Instead, we rely on a combination of receiver-driven flow control in the collective library, and coarse NIC-based flow control to limit outstanding data, and deep-buffer spine switches to absorb transient bursts. This is not wholly effective: zero-copy communication reduces opportunities for receiver feedback, increasing the likelihood of posting larger messages at once. We therefore see excessive buffer build-up. Our evaluation confirms this analysis: using zero-copy communication alone resulted in suboptimal performance. In contrast, copy-based communication inherently segments data into smaller chunks—due to temporary buffer size constraints—thereby providing implicit flow control.

CN: 增强的突发性影响了我们的拥塞控制策略。由于在多样化环境中难以正确调优，我们没有采用 DCQCN 等传统拥塞控制机制来限制交换机缓冲区占用 [8]。相反，我们依靠集合通信库中的接收方驱动流控、基于 NIC 的粗粒度流控（用于限制在途数据）以及深缓冲 spine 交换机的组合来吸收瞬时突发。这并非完全有效：零拷贝通信减少了接收方反馈的机会，增加了一次性投递更大消息的可能性。因此我们观察到过度的缓冲区堆积。我们的评估证实了这一分析：仅使用零拷贝通信会导致次优性能。相比之下，基于拷贝的通信由于临时缓冲区大小的约束，天然会将数据切分为更小的块，从而提供了隐式流控。

EN: To combine the advantages of both approaches, we employ zero-copy communication while internally partitioning data into smaller message segments and rate-limiting the number of segments in flight. To this end, we develop Dynamic Queue Pair Load Balancing (DQPLB) to configure the amount of outstanding data on a per-connection and per-topology basis, allowing higher limits for cross-DC or cross-AI-Zone links' bandwidth-delay product (BDP). This fine-grained control enables more effective control of buffer build-up, and therefore better sharing and fairness. Together with network load balancing improvements described in [8], we reduce switch buffer build-up by an order of magnitude.

CN: 为了结合两种方法的优点，我们采用零拷贝通信，同时在内部将数据切分为更小的消息分段，并对在途分段的数量进行限速。为此，我们开发了动态队列对负载均衡（DQPLB），按连接和按拓扑配置在途数据量，从而为跨数据中心或跨 AI 区域链路的带宽延迟积（BDP）设置更高的限制。这种细粒度控制能够更有效地控制缓冲区堆积，从而实现更好的共享与公平性。结合 [8] 中所述的网络负载均衡改进，我们将交换机缓冲区堆积降低了一个数量级。

### 4.2 DQPLB Design（DQPLB 设计）

EN: DQPLB controls the amount of data in the network using a small number of configurable parameters: the number of QPs per destination, the segment size, and the number of outstanding unacknowledged messages. The product of these three parameters should ideally match the BDP of the path between the sender and the receiver. Using smaller segment sizes also helps spread out the data in the network instead of bursting all at once. This design allows us to configure settings according to both the RTT of the path, and also the message sizes. We define four categories of connection types: within the same rack, cross-rack within the same zone, cross-zone within the same DC, and cross-DC. Based on the relative proximity of GPUs, we adjust the outstanding message limits for each connection.

CN: DQPLB 使用少量可配置参数来控制网络中的数据量：每个目的地的 QP 数量、分段大小以及在途未确认消息的数量。这三个参数的乘积在理想情况下应匹配发送方与接收方之间路径的 BDP。使用较小的分段大小还有助于将数据分散到网络中，而不是一次性全部突发出去。这一设计使我们能够根据路径的 RTT 以及消息大小来配置设置。我们定义了四类连接类型：同一机架内、同一区域内跨机架、同一数据中心内跨区域以及跨数据中心。根据 GPU 的相对邻近程度，我们为每条连接调整在途消息的限制。

EN: For connections with closer proximity, we use more conservative settings to accommodate the lower BDP. In contrast, for distant connections, we employ more aggressive configurations—such as higher number of data QPs and higher number of maximum number of outstanding messages—to better utilize the higher network BDP. Similarly, with smaller messages, we can be more aggressive in outstanding data to better utilize the network bandwidth.

CN: 对于邻近度更近的连接，我们使用更保守的设置以适配较低的 BDP。相反，对于较远的连接，我们采用更激进的配置——例如更多的数据 QP 数量以及更大的最大在途消息数——以更好地利用更高的网络 BDP。类似地，对于较小的消息，我们可以在在途数据方面更加激进，以更好地利用网络带宽。

EN: Figure 7: DQPLB design overview.

CN: 图 7：DQPLB 设计概览。

EN: As shown in Figure 7a, DQPLB uses one control QP and one or more data QPs. The control QP is responsible for exchanging memory addresses at the start of a collective operation, while the data QPs handle data transmission over the scale-out network. DQPLB achieves ordered message delivery across multiple data QPs by leveraging sequence numbering, immediate data encoding, and out-of-order message tracking. The algorithm distributes messages to data QPs in a round-robin fashion (Figure 7b). When a data QP's work queue reaches its configured limit, message transmission is paused until a completion queue element (CQE) is received on the corresponding completion queue (CQ). Upon receiving a CQE, the system resumes posting work queue elements (WQEs) for that QP. This dynamic allocation enables QPs with lower network contention to transmit data more efficiently, allowing them to handle a greater load.

CN: 如图 7a 所示，DQPLB 使用一个控制 QP 和一个或多个数据 QP。控制 QP 负责在集合通信操作开始时交换内存地址，而数据 QP 负责在横向扩展网络上的数据传输。DQPLB 通过利用序列号、立即数编码和乱序消息跟踪，在多个数据 QP 之间实现有序的消息传递。该算法以轮询（round-robin）方式将消息分发到数据 QP（图 7b）。当某个数据 QP 的工作队列达到其配置上限时，消息传输暂停，直到在相应的完成队列（CQ）上收到完成队列元素（CQE）。收到 CQE 后，系统恢复为该 QP 投递工作队列元素（WQE）。这种动态分配使网络争用较低的 QP 能够更高效地传输数据，从而处理更大的负载。

EN: To ensure ordered message delivery, we maintain two sequence counters: the sender tracks the next sequence number to transmit, while the receiver tracks the next expected sequence number.

CN: 为确保有序的消息传递，我们维护两个序列计数器：发送方跟踪下一个待发送的序列号，而接收方跟踪下一个期望收到的序列号。

EN: Figure 8: DQPLB performance with All-to-Allv.

CN: 图 8：DQPLB 在 All-to-Allv 下的性能。

EN: When transmitting messages in DQPLB mode, the sender utilizes the IBV_WR_RDMA_WRITE_WITH_IMM opcode for post send operations, instead of the standard IBV_WR_RDMA_WRITE. This enables the embedding of control information within the 32-bit immediate data field: bits 0–23 encode the sequential message number, bit 30 indicates fast path usage (explained in detail later), and bit 31 serves as a notification flag for the final write of a multi-packet message. When a message exceeds the maximum segment size, it is divided into multiple WQEs, with each partition assigned a consecutive sequence number; only the final fragment is marked with the notification bit in the immediate data field.

CN: 在 DQPLB 模式下传输消息时，发送方使用 IBV_WR_RDMA_WRITE_WITH_IMM 操作码执行 post send 操作，而非标准的 IBV_WR_RDMA_WRITE。这使得控制信息能够嵌入到 32 位的立即数字段中：第 0–23 位编码顺序消息号，第 30 位表示是否使用快速路径（稍后详细说明），第 31 位则作为多包消息最后一次写入的通知标志。当消息超过最大分段大小时，它被切分为多个 WQE，每个分片分配一个连续的序列号；只有最后一个分片在立即数字段中被标记通知位。

EN: On the receiver side, the algorithm supports out-of-order delivery by examining the immediate data of incoming messages to extract sequence numbers and notification flags. Out-of-order packets with sequence numbers beyond the next expected value are temporarily stored in a hash map indexed by sequence number, with boolean values indicating whether each packet carries a notification flag. The algorithm then applies a sliding window protocol that continuously checks for the arrival of the next expected sequence number; when it is received, the algorithm processes it along with any subsequent consecutive packets stored in the hash map, increments the notification counter for those marked with the notification bit, and removes the processed entries from the hash map. This approach guarantees that notifications are triggered only after all preceding messages in the sequence have been received, thereby preserving strict ordering semantics even when packets arrive out of order due to distribution across multiple data QPs.

CN: 在接收方一侧，该算法通过检查入站消息的立即数来提取序列号和通知标志，从而支持乱序投递。序列号超过下一个期望值的乱序数据包被临时存储在一个以序列号为索引的哈希映射中，布尔值表示每个数据包是否携带通知标志。随后该算法应用滑动窗口协议，持续检查下一个期望序列号是否到达；当收到它时，算法将它与哈希映射中存储的任何后续连续数据包一起处理，为那些带有通知位的数据包递增通知计数器，并将已处理的条目从哈希映射中移除。这种方法保证只有在序列中所有前置消息都已收到之后才会触发通知，从而即使数据包因分发到多个数据 QP 而乱序到达，也能保持严格的顺序语义。

EN: The algorithm also incorporates a fast path optimization for high-frequency operations, enabling messages to bypass the multi-QP distribution and be sent directly on a dedicated data QP. On the receiver side, these messages are processed by directly incrementing the receive-next sequence counter and updating notification counts, eliminating the need for out-of-order tracking. The fast path minimizes the CPU overhead of each RDMA operation, especially useful for algorithms with multiple small to medium RDMA operations such as the inference use case.

CN: 该算法还为高频操作集成了快速路径优化，使消息能够绕过多 QP 分发，直接在专用的数据 QP 上发送。在接收方一侧，这些消息通过直接递增"下一个接收"序列计数器并更新通知计数来处理，从而无需乱序跟踪。快速路径最小化了每次 RDMA 操作的 CPU 开销，尤其适用于包含多个中小型 RDMA 操作的算法，例如推理用例。

### 4.3 DQPLB performance（DQPLB 性能）

EN: We first look at an All-to-Allv (All-to-All with varying message sizes) experiment with 32, 64, and 128 GPUs – this is one of the most network intensive traffic profiles. With our baseline, we use NCCL with NVLink support, whereas with DQPLB, we do not use NVLink. The reason being that at the time of running the experiments, NVLink support in our library was not mature. Furthermore, DQPLB is targeted towards network congestion issues, which are not relevant to host interconnects, which are significantly faster. Running DQPLB without NVLink therefore allows us to see how it performs in the network without potential bottlenecks being hidden or compensated by the faster host interconnects. We ran our experiments with 400 Gbps NICs. We run all our experiments emulating perfect spray underneath. We configure DQPLB with 128KB in flight with 1 QP – this gives us fine-grained control over the data in flight. DQPLB is able to maintain close to maximum throughput. Note that NVLink improves the bus bandwidth beyond 50GBps because of the faster interconnect within hosts. Figure 8 shows that DQPLB achieves close to 50GBps at a fraction of peak buffer utilization – with almost a 90% reduction at 32 GPUs to about 75% reduction at 128 GPUs.

CN: 我们首先考察一个使用 32、64 和 128 个 GPU 的 All-to-Allv（消息大小可变的 All-to-All）实验——这是网络负载最重的流量形态之一。在我们的基线中，我们使用带 NVLink 支持的 NCCL，而在 DQPLB 中，我们不使用 NVLink。原因在于进行实验时，我们库中的 NVLink 支持尚未成熟。此外，DQPLB 针对的是网络拥塞问题，而这些问题与主机互连无关，因为主机互连的速度显著更快。因此，在不用 NVLink 的情况下运行 DQPLB，使我们能够观察它在网络中的表现，而不让潜在的瓶颈被更快的主机互连所掩盖或补偿。我们使用 400 Gbps 的 NIC 进行实验。我们所有的实验都在底层模拟 perfect spray。我们将 DQPLB 配置为 128KB 在途数据并使用 1 个 QP——这使我们对在途数据拥有细粒度的控制。DQPLB 能够维持接近最大的吞吐量。请注意，由于主机内部互连更快，NVLink 将总线带宽提升到超过 50GBps。图 8 显示，DQPLB 在峰值缓冲区占用率的一小部分下实现了接近 50GBps 的性能——在 32 个 GPU 时几乎降低了 90%，在 128 个 GPU 时约降低了 75%。

EN: Figure 9: 256 GPU AllGather bus bandwidth and buffer before and after tuning.

CN: 图 9：调优前后 256 GPU AllGather 的总线带宽与缓冲区。

EN: Next, we test with a 256 GPU AllGather that uses a ring implementation with rail-based setting (no NVLink). This is a representative experiment size as actual training workloads shard the cluster into much smaller, localized Process Groups (typically 32–256 GPUs, especially for MoE All-to-Allv operations). Although ring-based collectives do not have incast traffic patterns, the mix of RTTs will still result in momentary bursts at the receiver. First we run this with default settings, and then we tune the segment size and the number of QPs. The default is 512KB with 16 QPs. We tune this to 64KB segments, but only 2 QPs for in-rack scope, 4 QPs for cross-rack but within the same datacenter, and 64 QPs across datacenters. QP counts are chosen based on BDP of the path, which in this context is entirely a function of RTT. More BDP requires more proportionately more QPs. Figure 9 shows a 72% decrease in peak buffering, at no performance penalty at 1GB message sizes, and a 13% improvement in bus bandwidth for 8MB message sizes.

CN: 接下来，我们用一个 256 GPU 的 AllGather 进行测试，它使用基于 rail 的环形实现（无 NVLink）。这是一个具有代表性的实验规模，因为实际的训练工作负载会将集群划分为更小的、局部化的进程组（通常为 32–256 个 GPU，尤其是对于 MoE 的 All-to-Allv 操作）。尽管基于环的集合通信没有 incast 流量模式，但混合的 RTT 仍会在接收方产生瞬时的突发。我们首先使用默认设置运行，然后调优分段大小和 QP 数量。默认配置是 512KB 和 16 个 QP。我们将其调优为 64KB 分段，但机架内范围仅使用 2 个 QP，跨机架但在同一数据中心内使用 4 个 QP，跨数据中心使用 64 个 QP。QP 数量根据路径的 BDP 选择，而在这种情境下 BDP 完全取决于 RTT。更大的 BDP 需要相应更多的 QP。图 9 显示峰值缓冲降低了 72%，在 1GB 消息大小下没有任何性能损失，而在 8MB 消息大小下总线带宽提升了 13%。

EN: First we note that the buffer wins are slightly smaller than with a more intensive All-to-Allv; this is because of the impact of cross-DC traffic. Larger BDP paths and the inability to arbitrarily reduce segment size (as mentioned previously) means that we need to burst somewhat, reducing the wins. However, smaller segments does offer some level of pacing benefit. Secondly, we see a performance improvement for the smaller, 8MB collective size; this is likely due to the latency of lower buffering.

CN: 首先我们注意到，缓冲区方面的收益比负载更重的 All-to-Allv 略小；这是因为跨数据中心流量的影响。更大的 BDP 路径以及无法任意减小分段大小（如前所述）意味着我们需要一定程度的突发，从而减少了收益。然而，更小的分段确实提供了一定程度的节流（pacing）收益。其次，我们看到在较小的 8MB 集合通信规模下性能有所提升；这很可能归因于更低缓冲带来的延迟降低。

## 5 Operational Tooling（运维工具）

### 5.1 Fault Localization（故障定位）

EN: Due to the synchronized execution of LLM training, a single machine crash can stall or terminate the entire training job. In production environments, engineers often spend hours or even days identifying and isolating the faulty machine, during which additional failures may occur. To address this operational challenge, we developed Fault Analyzer (Figure 10), an automated system that analyzes training job failures and efficiently localizes faulty machines in real time.

CN: 由于 LLM 训练是同步执行的，单台机器崩溃就可能停滞或终止整个训练作业。在生产环境中，工程师往往要花费数小时甚至数天来定位并隔离故障机器，而在此期间又可能出现更多故障。为应对这一运维难题，我们开发了 Fault Analyzer（图 10），这是一套自动化系统，能够分析训练作业故障并实时高效地定位故障机器。

EN: Figure 10: Overall architecture for the Fault Analyzer.

CN: 图 10：Fault Analyzer 的整体架构。

EN: In an LLM training job, many collective operations can execute simultaneously. The primary challenge is to identify the root-cause collective from this large set of operations when the job fails. Multi-dimensional model parallelism introduces complicated dependency between collectives, forming intricate execution graphs. When a single collective hangs, it can trigger cascading effects that impact all dependent collectives. It is impractical to exhaustively analyze all collective operations due to the massive volume of logs, the large number of participating machines, and the high algorithm complexity. Therefore, the design goal of Fault Analyzer is to identify the culprit collective operations in a fast and scalable manner.

CN: 在 LLM 训练作业中，大量集合通信操作会同时执行。首要挑战是在作业失败时从这一大批操作中找出根因集合通信。多维模型并行给集合通信之间引入了复杂的依赖关系，构成错综复杂的执行图。当某个集合通信挂起时，会引发级联效应，波及所有依赖它的集合通信。由于日志体量庞大、参与机器众多、算法复杂度高，穷举分析所有集合通信操作是不现实的。因此，Fault Analyzer 的设计目标是以快速且可扩展的方式识别出肇事集合通信操作。

EN: During job execution, it incrementally constructs an inter-collective dependency graph in real time. The graph dynamically removes completed collectives and adds newly scheduled ones. The system continuously monitors unfinished collectives that have no outstanding dependencies and flags them as culprit candidates if their execution time exceeds a predefined timeout threshold. Fault Analyzer localizes the specific failing node associated with each culprit collective.

CN: 在作业执行期间，它实时增量地构建集合通信间的依赖图。该依赖图动态地移除已完成的集合通信，并加入新调度的集合通信。系统持续监控那些没有未完成依赖的未完成集合通信，一旦其执行时间超过预设的超时阈值，就将其标记为肇事候选。Fault Analyzer 进一步定位与每个肇事集合通信相关联的具体故障节点。

EN: When multiple collectives hang simultaneously, only a subset of them are true root causes while the remaining ones are blocked while waiting for their dependencies to complete. To construct the inter-collective dependency graph, we rely on two assumptions: (1) When a job has been stalled for a long duration, all normal collectives should have finished. (2) If a collective has been scheduled but has not yet started, it must be waiting, either directly or indirectly, on other started but unfinished collectives on the same node. In other words, an unstarted collective depends on all such unfinished collectives. Although the second assumption is empirical, we find it highly effective in practice. An unstarted collective may be stalled because it shares the same CUDA stream as an unfinished collective, has CUDA event or data dependencies on it, or because the unfinished collective has exhausted critical hardware resources.

CN: 当多个集合通信同时挂起时，其中只有一部分是真正的根因，其余的都是因等待其依赖完成而被阻塞。为了构建集合通信间的依赖图，我们依据两条假设：(1) 当作业已停滞很长时间时，所有正常的集合通信都应当已经完成。(2) 如果一个集合通信已被调度但尚未开始，那么它必定直接或间接地依赖于同一节点上其他已开始但未完成的集合通信。换言之，一个尚未开始的集合通信依赖于所有这类未完成的集合通信。尽管第二条假设是经验性的，但我们在实践中发现它非常有效。一个未开始的集合通信之所以停滞，可能是因为它与某个未完成的集合通信共享同一 CUDA 流、对其存在 CUDA 事件或数据依赖，或者因为该未完成的集合通信耗尽了对关键硬件资源。

EN: Based on these assumptions, we iterate over every node with stalled collectives, analyze the dependency relationships among collectives on the node, and construct a global dependency directed acyclic graph (DAG). Collectives without outgoing dependencies in the DAG are identified as culprit collectives. Algorithm 1 presents the detailed procedure.

CN: 基于这些假设，我们遍历每个存在停滞集合通信的节点，分析该节点上集合通信之间的依赖关系，并构建一张全局的依赖有向无环图（DAG）。DAG 中没有出边依赖的集合通信被识别为肇事集合通信。算法 1 给出了详细过程。

EN: Algorithm 1: Constructing the Dependency DAG Among Hanging Collectives. Require: Mtimeout — machines that have timeout collectives. Ensure: Gdep — dependency DAG between collectives.

CN: 算法 1：构建挂起集合通信之间的依赖 DAG。输入（Require）：Mtimeout——存在超时集合通信的机器集合。输出（Ensure）：Gdep——集合通信之间的依赖 DAG。

```
1: procedure BuildDAG(Mtimeout)
2:   Gdep ← ∅
3:   for all node M ∈ Mtimeout do
4:     CM ← getTimeoutCollectives(M)
5:     CA ← ∅    ⊲ Started yet incomplete collectives（已开始但未完成的集合通信）
6:     for all collective C ∈ CM do
7:       if kernelStart(C) and ¬KernelEnd(C) then
8:         CA ← CA ∪ {C}
9:       end if
10:    end for
11:    for all collective C ∈ CM do
12:      if ¬KernelStarted(C) then
13:        Gdep[C] ← CA    ⊲ C depends on CA（C 依赖于 CA）
14:      end if
15:    end for
16:  end for
17:  return Gdep
18: end procedure
```

EN: We further illustrate the procedure with a simple example. On Node 1, Collective A has started but remains unfinished, while Collective B is scheduled but unstarted. On Node 2, Collective B is unfinished and Collective C is unstarted. Following the algorithm, we add a dependency from B to A on Node 1 and from C to B on Node 2. Since A has no outgoing dependencies, it is identified as the culprit collective.

CN: 我们再用一个简单例子说明该过程。在节点 1 上，集合通信 A 已开始但仍未完成，而集合通信 B 已被调度但尚未开始。在节点 2 上，集合通信 B 未完成，集合通信 C 尚未开始。按照该算法，我们在节点 1 上添加从 B 到 A 的依赖，在节点 2 上添加从 C 到 B 的依赖。由于 A 没有出边依赖，它被识别为肇事集合通信。

EN: To track whether a collective is scheduled, started and completed, we instrumented CCLX with tracing instructions at per-collective granularity, called CollTrace. CollTrace forms the foundation of our dependency analysis and provides fine-grained visibility into collective execution states.

CN: 为了跟踪集合通信是否已被调度、开始和完成，我们以每个集合通信为粒度在 CCLX 中插桩了追踪指令，称为 CollTrace。CollTrace 构成了我们依赖分析的基础，并提供了对集合通信执行状态的细粒度可见性。

EN: For each culprit collective, we identify the responsible rank and mark the node hosting that rank as faulty using the following procedure. First, we check per-rank arguments (e.g., operation type, element count, etc) in the collective. If a rank provides inconsistent or invalid arguments relative to its peers, we flag it as faulty. Next, we check whether all ranks have launched the kernel and entered the execution phase. Any node that fails to start the kernel is identified as faulty. Subsequently, we detect network-level failures by examining errors thrown between communicating rank pairs. If multiple errors are observed, we select the rank involved in the largest number of failed pairwise communications as the most likely faulty ranks.

CN: 对于每个肇事集合通信，我们按以下过程识别负有责任的 rank，并将承载该 rank 的节点标记为故障节点。首先，检查该集合通信中各 rank 的参数（例如操作类型、元素数量等）。如果某个 rank 提供的参数与其对等 rank 不一致或无效，我们就将其标记为故障。接着，检查所有 rank 是否都已启动内核并进入执行阶段，任何未能启动内核的节点都被识别为故障。随后，通过检查相互通信的 rank 对之间抛出的错误来检测网络层面的故障。如果观测到多个错误，我们选取参与最多失败成对通信的 rank，作为最可能的故障 rank。

EN: If the above checks reveal no issues and no errors are thrown, we construct a wait-for dependency graph based on proxy operation states. In a collective operation, before the sender write data chunks into the receiver's HBM, the receiver uses the proxy thread to send control messages describing the destination buffer metadata to the sender. The sender then initiates data transmission from its proxy thread. We model two types of dependencies: (1) control-message wait dependencies: The sender is blocked waiting for the receiver's control message containing destination buffer metadata. (2) data wait dependencies: The receiver is blocked waiting for the sender's data transfer. The Fault Analyzer builds the wait-for dependency graph based on these two dependencies by checking the proxy operation states. Nodes without outgoing dependency are considered candidate faulty nodes. We present two brief case studies to go through the fault localization procedure.

CN: 如果上述检查未发现问题且没有抛出任何错误，我们就基于 proxy 操作状态构建一个等待依赖图（wait-for dependency graph）。在一次集合通信操作中，发送方在将数据块写入接收方的 HBM 之前，接收方会使用 proxy 线程向发送方发送描述目标缓冲区元数据的控制消息，发送方随后从自己的 proxy 线程发起数据传输。我们建模两类依赖：(1) 控制消息等待依赖：发送方被阻塞，等待接收方携带目标缓冲区元数据的控制消息。(2) 数据等待依赖：接收方被阻塞，等待发送方的数据传输。Fault Analyzer 通过检查 proxy 操作状态，基于这两类依赖构建等待依赖图。没有出边依赖的节点被视为候选故障节点。下面通过两个简要案例来走一遍故障定位过程。

EN: Model code bug: Bugs introduced during model development may eventually manifest as NCCL timeouts in PyTorch, which are often difficult and time-consuming to debug. In a 4K-GPU training job, approximately 500 collectives experienced timeouts. Fault Analyzer automatically constructed the inter-collective dependency graph and identified the root-cause collective as the final collective in a tensor-parallel (TP) process group. By checking the kernel execution status across hosts, we observed that one rank had not launched the corresponding collective kernel, while all other ranks had started execution. This rank was therefore flagged as the likely culprit. With this insight, the developer traced the issue to a software bug that caused the specific rank to hang before scheduling the final collective.

CN: 模型代码缺陷：模型开发过程中引入的缺陷最终可能表现为 PyTorch 中的 NCCL 超时，这类问题往往难以调试且耗时。在一个 4K-GPU 训练作业中，大约有 500 个集合通信发生了超时。Fault Analyzer 自动构建了集合通信间依赖图，并识别出根因集合通信是张量并行（TP）进程组中的最后一个集合通信。通过检查各主机上的内核执行状态，我们观测到一个 rank 尚未启动相应的集合通信内核，而其他所有 rank 都已开始执行，因此该 rank 被标记为最可能的肇事者。凭借这一线索，开发者将问题定位到一个软件缺陷——它导致该特定 rank 在调度最后一个集合通信之前就挂起了。

EN: Hardware failure: In another training job spanning 8K GPUs, the job experienced repeated restarts accompanied by hundreds of collectives timeouts. Fault Analyzer first aggregated the collective and network operation traces, and built the inter-collective dependency graph. After analysis, it found the last AllReduce in a data-parallel (DP) process group as the culprit collective. Network-level traces did not report explicit communication errors. We therefore constructed a wait-for dependency graph based on proxy operation states. The analysis revealed that one host had stopped transmitting data to its peer, and further investigation confirmed that a malfunctioning NIC on that host was the root cause.

CN: 硬件故障：在另一个横跨 8K GPU 的训练作业中，作业反复重启，并伴随数百个集合通信超时。Fault Analyzer 首先聚合集合通信和网络操作的追踪记录，并构建集合通信间依赖图。分析后，它发现数据并行（DP）进程组中的最后一个 AllReduce 是肇事集合通信。网络层追踪未报告显式的通信错误。因此，我们基于 proxy 操作状态构建了等待依赖图。分析揭示某台主机已停止向其对等方传输数据，进一步排查确认该主机上的一块故障 NIC 才是根因。

### 5.2 Performance Observability（性能可观测性）

EN: Unexpected network latency or congestion can severely compromise overall job performance. Conventional profiling tools often lack the comprehensive context spanning the workload and communication layers needed to isolate network issues and to assess the impact based on whether the affected communication are exposed. We developed a lightweight PerfProfiler to provide real-time, fine-grained, and context-aware performance monitoring. PerfProfiler is designed for minimal CPU overhead and flexible sampling, ensuring that the profiling overhead is negligible and allows it to run continuously throughout the entire training process. It has two primary modules:

CN: 意外的网络延迟或拥塞会严重损害整体作业性能。传统的性能分析工具往往缺乏横跨工作负载层与通信层的完整上下文，而这正是隔离网络问题、并根据受影响的通信是否暴露来评估其影响所必需的。我们开发了轻量级的 PerfProfiler，以提供实时、细粒度且具备上下文感知的性能监控。PerfProfiler 的设计目标是最小的 CPU 开销与灵活的采样，确保性能分析开销可忽略不计，从而能够在整个训练过程中持续运行。它包含两个主要模块：

EN: AlgoProfiler: It gathers essential timestamps at the collective algorithm layer. It then dissects and measures the latency of various collective stages, including buffer registration, control message synchronization, and data transfer. These measured durations help pinpoint the bottleneck stage in a slow collective; for instance, determining if the delay is due to memory registration (a specific overhead for zero-copy collectives), network congestion (extended data transfer), or a slow rank (prolonged message synchronization).

CN: AlgoProfiler：它在集合通信算法层采集关键时间戳，然后剖析并测量集合通信各阶段的延迟，包括缓冲区注册、控制消息同步和数据传输。这些测得的时长有助于定位一个缓慢集合通信中的瓶颈阶段；例如，判断延迟是源于内存注册（零拷贝集合通信的特定开销）、网络拥塞（数据传输延长）还是慢 rank（消息同步拖长）。

EN: Figure 11: Comparison between a GPU communication stack and its CPU emulation.

CN: 图 11：GPU 通信栈与其 CPU 仿真之间的对比。

EN: SlowRankDetector: Monitors per-rank network efficiency at the CTran transport layer and identifies slow ranks. It captures the latency of individual RDMA packets by recording timestamps for the Work Queue Entry (WQE) issuing and completion events. Subsequently, it calculates the bus bandwidth for each rank over a rolling window and reports any slow peer connection below a threshold.

CN: SlowRankDetector：在 CTran 传输层监控各 rank 的网络效率并识别慢 rank。它通过记录工作队列条目（WQE）下发与完成事件的时间戳，来捕获单个 RDMA 数据包的延迟。随后，它在滚动窗口上计算每个 rank 的总线带宽，并上报任何低于阈值的慢速对等连接。

EN: Case Study: The model engineer noticed a significant training performance drop during certain iterations. The symptom was slow pipeline parallelism point-to-point communication in some per-rank traces. The initial step was to ascertain whether the cause was slow communication/network or a different type of non-communication delay. By examining the synchronization duration sampled from the affected iteration, we observed that some ranks reported a 100x longer delay, while the data transfer duration was normal. Focusing on the peers with this extended synchronization led us to the root cause: the ranks were blocked by a slow I/O operation, classifying them as a "slow rank".

CN: 案例研究：模型工程师注意到在某些迭代期间训练性能显著下降。症状是一些逐 rank 追踪中流水线并行的点对点通信缓慢。第一步是确定原因是通信/网络缓慢，还是其他类型的非通信延迟。通过检查受影响迭代中采样的同步时长，我们观测到某些 rank 报告的延迟长了 100 倍，而数据传输时长正常。聚焦于这些同步延长的对等方，我们找到了根因：这些 rank 被一次缓慢的 I/O 操作阻塞，从而被归类为"慢 rank"。

### 5.3 Distributed CPU emulation（分布式 CPU 仿真）

EN: Testing changes to the collective communication stack at full 100K+ scale is prohibitively expensive. Control-plane regressions can idle tens of thousands of GPUs, yet many of the most costly failures manifest only under extreme concurrency and topology scale. To shift these risks earlier in the development cycle, we built a distributed CPU emulation framework that executes unmodified communication and PyTorch Distributed binaries on an existing CPU cluster. The framework provides early, high-signal validation of control-plane correctness and scale behavior.

CN: 在完整的 100K+ 规模上测试集合通信栈的改动代价极其高昂。控制面的回归可能让数万块 GPU 空转，而许多代价最高的故障只在极端并发和拓扑规模下才会显现。为了把这些风险前移到开发周期的更早阶段，我们构建了一个分布式 CPU 仿真框架，在现有的 CPU 集群上运行未经修改的通信库和 PyTorch Distributed 二进制文件。该框架能够对控制面正确性与规模行为提供早期、高信噪比的验证。

EN: GPU communication libraries cannot run on CPUs out of the box because it depends on GPU- and RDMA-specific user-space stacks even during initialization. Our approach in Figure 11 preserves the production binaries and swaps only the dependency surfaces. We provide drop-in replacements for libcudart, libcuda, libnvidia-ml, and libibverbs; redirect resolution with LD_LIBRARY_PATH; and share common emulator state via a small library loaded with LD_PRELOAD. This design keeps the application and PyTorch, and the communication libraries unmodified.

CN: GPU 通信库无法直接在 CPU 上开箱即用地运行，因为即使在初始化阶段，它也依赖 GPU 与 RDMA 专用的用户态协议栈。图 11 中我们的做法是保留生产二进制文件，只替换其依赖表面。我们为 libcudart、libcuda、libnvidia-ml 和 libibverbs 提供了可直接替换的替身；用 LD_LIBRARY_PATH 重定向符号解析；并通过一个经 LD_PRELOAD 加载的小型库共享通用的仿真器状态。这一设计使应用程序、PyTorch 以及通信库都保持原样，无需修改。

EN: We target control-plane fidelity, not dataplane performance. The emulator reproduces API-level semantics exercised during CCLX initialization and bring-up: device enumeration; stable GPU-memory identities and handle lifetimes; topology and P2P capability queries; verbs object creation and state transitions; and correct error propagation and teardown behavior.

CN: 我们的目标是控制面的保真度，而非数据面性能。该仿真器复现了 CCLX 初始化与启动过程中所涉及的 API 层语义：设备枚举；稳定的 GPU 内存标识与句柄生命周期；拓扑与 P2P 能力查询；verbs 对象的创建与状态迁移；以及正确的错误传播与拆除行为。

EN: CUDA allocations are backed by pinned host memory. We maintain stable handle/pointer translation so the communication library observes consistent identities and lifetimes across allocation, IPC-handle use, and teardown. We have emulated the initialization-relevant subset of libibverbs.so sufficient for CCLX bring-up: device/context discovery, protection domains, memory-region metadata, QP objects, and event handling. We exercise CCLX connection-graph construction, resource scaling behavior, and error handling under realistic rank counts. We configure each CPU host to present eight virtual GPUs and run one rank per virtual device, enabling 96K-rank experiments on 12K CPU servers.

CN: CUDA 分配由钉扎（pinned）主机内存承载。我们维护稳定的句柄/指针转换，使通信库在分配、IPC 句柄使用和拆除过程中观察到一致的标识与生命周期。我们已仿真了 libibverbs.so 中与初始化相关的、足以支持 CCLX 启动的子集：设备/上下文发现、保护域、内存区域元数据、QP 对象和事件处理。我们在贴近现实的 rank 数量下测试了 CCLX 连接图构建、资源伸缩行为和错误处理。我们将每个 CPU 主机配置为呈现 8 个虚拟 GPU，每个虚拟设备运行一个 rank，从而能在 12K 台 CPU 服务器上进行 96K-rank 规模的实验。

EN: This framework enabled early, high-signal validation and performance modeling of our scalable initialization techniques (Section 3.2), allowing us to iterate quickly. It also reliably exposed scale-triggered control-plane issues that are effectively invisible at smaller scales. For example, beyond 64K ranks we saw bootstrap fan-in hit OS-level limits (e.g., ephemeral port pressure and accept-queue overflow under connection storms), and used the framework to validate burst-tolerant retry/backoff behavior. We also identified initialization paths whose state unintentionally grew with the number of peers (e.g., eager QP and metadata provisioning), leading to prohibitive memory growth during global communicator creation and directly motivating on-demand provisioning. Finally, we observed quadratic amplification in initialization-time telemetry when each rank emitted per-peer metadata, creating O(N²) bursts that overwhelmed downstream systems and motivating aggregation and rate-limiting during bring-up.

CN: 该框架为我们可扩展的初始化技术（第 3.2 节）提供了早期、高信噪比的验证与性能建模，使我们能够快速迭代。它还可靠地暴露了在小规模下几乎不可见的、由规模触发的控制面问题。例如，超过 64K rank 后，我们观察到 bootstrap 的扇入触发了操作系统层面的限制（例如连接风暴下的临时端口压力与 accept 队列溢出），并利用该框架验证了抗突发重试/退避行为。我们还发现某些初始化路径的状态会无意间随对等方数量增长（例如急切的 QP 与元数据预分配），导致全局通信子创建期间内存增长到难以接受的程度，这直接促使我们采用按需预分配。最后，我们观察到初始化阶段遥测的二次方放大现象——当每个 rank 都发送逐对等方元数据时，会产生 O(N²) 的突发，压垮下游系统，这促使我们在启动期间进行聚合与限速。

### 6.2 Limitations（局限）

EN: Initialization: While CCLX significantly mitigates initialization overhead at our current scale, this global state exchange strategy faces fundamental scaling limits as clusters transition toward next-generation regional deployments. Future efficiency gains will likely require moving toward hierarchical initialization or decentralized, peer-to-peer discovery mechanisms where ranks only maintain localized connectivity metadata. Due to development complexity and production deployment timelines, we did not explore these decentralized architectures in the current fabric. Furthermore, while fault-tolerance frameworks like torchft [25] successfully reduce the blast radius of individual host failures, integrating decentralized initialization with robust replica-based recovery models remains an open necessity for scaling beyond the 100K-GPU threshold.

CN: 初始化（Initialization）：虽然 CCLX 在我们当前的规模下显著缓解了初始化开销，但随着集群向下一代区域性部署演进，这种全局状态交换策略面临根本性的扩展限制。未来要提升效率，很可能需要转向分层初始化或去中心化的点对点发现机制，即各 rank 仅维护本地化的连接元数据。由于开发复杂度和生产部署时间表，我们在当前的网络架构中并未探索这些去中心化架构。此外，尽管 torchft [25] 等容错框架成功缩小了单台主机故障的影响范围，但将去中心化初始化与健壮的基于副本的恢复模型相集成，仍是突破 100K GPU 规模阈值的未决需求。

EN: Emulation: We rely on our distributed CPU emulation framework to expose scale-triggered control-plane regressions early in the development cycle. However, as designed, this framework prioritizes control-plane fidelity rather than data-plane performance. It successfully validates initialization mechanics, connection-graph construction, and buffer allocation behaviors under realistic rank counts, but it does not model raw data-plane execution. Achieving faithful, end-to-end data-plane emulation at full production scale (100K+) remains an open question for the community.

CN: 仿真（Emulation）：我们依赖分布式 CPU 仿真框架，在开发周期早期暴露由规模触发的控制平面回归问题。不过，按照设计，该框架优先保障控制平面的保真度，而非数据平面的性能。它能够成功验证初始化机制、连接图构建以及真实 rank 数量下的缓冲区分配行为，但不对原始数据平面执行进行建模。在全生产规模（100K+）下实现忠实、端到端的数据平面仿真，仍是社区悬而未决的问题。

EN: Fault Localization: Emerging system optimization paradigms are beginning to introduce visibility challenges for our analytical fault localization and profiling infrastructure. In pursuit of peak hardware utilization, the industry is rapidly transitioning toward highly optimized, fused GPU kernels[22] that interweave computation directly with communication operations. This architectural blending inherently obscures the precise, deterministic boundaries of individual collective operations. Consequently, such fusion poses a significant challenge to tracing tools like CollTrace, which structurally rely on distinct per-collective execution states to construct accurate inter-collective dependency graphs. As static analytical models face diminishing visibility due to opaque, fused runtime traces, our reliance on explicit wait-for state tracking must adapt. Future diagnostic architectures will likely need to explore how AI-assisted debugging can complement or replace static graph-based models to aggregated performance data.

CN: 故障定位（Fault Localization）：新兴的系统优化范式开始对我们的分析型故障定位与性能剖析基础设施带来可见性挑战。为追求硬件的峰值利用率，业界正迅速转向高度优化的融合 GPU 内核[22]，这些内核将计算直接与通信操作交织在一起。这种架构上的融合本质上模糊了单个集合操作之间精确、确定的边界。因此，此类融合对 CollTrace 等追踪工具构成重大挑战——这些工具在结构上依赖每个集合操作各自独立的执行状态，来构建准确的集合间依赖图。随着静态分析模型因不透明、融合的运行时追踪而可见性不断下降，我们对显式等待状态（wait-for）跟踪的依赖必须随之调整。未来的诊断架构很可能需要探索 AI 辅助调试如何能够补充或替代基于静态图的模型，以应对聚合的性能数据。

EN: Production Ablations: Most of our CCLX improvements are validated solely in communication-focused benchmarks. Conducting live, full-cluster network ablations in a production environment connecting over 100K GPUs is rendered impossible by prohibitive resource, scheduling, and financial constraints. Typically, a variety of infrastructure features - including architectural changes, compute kernels, communication enhancement, and configuration changes - are bundled into a single package deployed on a pre-defined date. Each of these features is tested at a smaller scale and gated by a configuration flag. Once pre-training begins, the job usually runs for several weeks, which may pause and resume from checkpoints many times. A feature can be disabled quickly if it actively causes issues. For features that run smoothly, detailed telemetry is often turned off to save overhead. Although it is challenging to validate their isolated effectiveness in production jobs, we can infer their impact indirectly. For example, the fast initialization feature was kept active to ensure training could restart within its time budget after any interruption, and all collectives were successfully hidden behind computation when DQPLB was turned on.

CN: 生产消融（Production Ablations）：我们的大部分 CCLX 改进仅在以通信为中心的基准测试中得到验证。在连接超过 10 万块 GPU 的生产环境中进行实时的、全集群网络消融实验，因资源、调度和财务方面的巨大限制而无法实现。通常，各种基础设施特性——包括架构变更、计算内核、通信增强以及配置变更——会被打包进一个在预定日期部署的单一软件包中。每个特性都在较小规模下测试，并由一个配置标志（flag）控制。一旦预训练开始，作业通常会运行数周，期间可能多次暂停并从检查点恢复。如果某个特性主动引发问题，可以快速禁用它。对于运行顺畅的特性，往往会关闭详细遥测以节省开销。尽管在生产作业中验证它们各自孤立的有效性颇具挑战，但我们仍可间接推断其影响。例如，快速初始化特性保持启用，以确保训练在任何中断后都能在其时间预算内重新启动；而开启 DQPLB 后，所有集合操作都被成功隐藏在计算之后。

## 7 Related Works（相关工作）

EN: There is an extensive body of recent research on collective communication libraries [15, 16, 28, 29, 38] as well as higher-layer communication frameworks [3, 21, 27, 30]. Our work on CCLX does not claim inherent superiority over these specialized approaches; rather, it focuses on the specific operational and architectural learnings required to sustain training at the 100K scale. Our contributions are complementary to existing GPU communication research, providing a bridge between collective optimizations and the practical requirements of massive-scale production fabrics.

CN: 近年来，围绕集合通信库 [15, 16, 28, 29, 38] 以及更高层通信框架 [3, 21, 27, 30] 已有大量研究。我们在 CCLX 上的工作并不声称本质上优于这些专门方案；相反，它聚焦于在 100K 规模下维持训练所需的具体运维与架构经验。我们的贡献与现有的 GPU 通信研究互补，在集合优化与大规模生产网络的实用需求之间架起了一座桥梁。

EN: Initialization: Initialization has historically received limited attention in the GPU-based distributed training literature, primarily because its overhead only becomes prohibitive at the large scale. Conversely, the HPC community has long addressed similar scaling bottlenecks in CPU clusters through frameworks such as PMIx [2]. As GPU clusters reach unprecedented scales, this issue has resurfaced as a primary concern, evidenced by MegaScale [13], which operates at 10K scale, and NVIDIA's introduction of the scalable initialization API [23]. Our experience suggests that this domain requires continuous optimization; as clusters scale, the compounding effects of increased interruption frequency and initialization time can significantly degrade training efficiency.

CN: 初始化（Initialization）：在基于 GPU 的分布式训练文献中，初始化历来受到的关注有限，这主要是因为其开销只有在超大规模下才会变得难以承受。相比之下，HPC 社区长期以来通过 PMIx [2] 等框架解决 CPU 集群中类似的扩展瓶颈。随着 GPU 集群达到前所未有的规模，这一问题重新成为首要关切，这从运行在 10K 规模的 MegaScale [13] 以及 NVIDIA 推出的可扩展初始化 API [23] 可见一斑。我们的经验表明，这一领域需要持续优化；随着集群规模扩大，中断频率增加与初始化时间增长的复合效应会显著降低训练效率。

EN: Fault localization: There have been many works on fault localization for AI infrastructure. For example, Minder [4] and PerfTracker [10] focus on identifying stragglers and gray failures by analyzing machine health and applying differential observability by leveraging temporal and spatial similarity across workers. To debug the complex interactions between workers, Mycroft [5] and Aegis [6] intercept and trace dependencies within collective communication primitives to pinpoint faults. BiAn [33] leverages LLMs to automate the reasoning over monitoring logs for rapid root cause analysis. From our experiences, we find simple and scalable algorithms that construct inter-collective dependency graphs are more effective for fault localization at this scale.

CN: 故障定位（Fault localization）：针对 AI 基础设施的故障定位已有大量工作。例如，Minder [4] 和 PerfTracker [10] 专注于通过分析机器健康状况、并利用 worker 之间的时空相似性进行差分可观测性，来识别慢节点（straggler）和灰色故障（gray failure）。为调试 worker 之间复杂的交互，Mycroft [5] 和 Aegis [6] 拦截并追踪集合通信原语内部的依赖关系以精确定位故障。BiAn [33] 利用 LLM 自动化地对监控日志进行推理，以实现快速根因分析。根据我们的经验，构建集合间依赖图的简单且可扩展的算法，在此规模下的故障定位中更为有效。

EN: Transport: There are two lines of work in AI transport: load-balancing, and congestion management. They are complementary approaches to solving the overall problem of congestion, buffering, and latency in the network. The default solution for load-balancing of ECMP assumes large flow entropy. However, AI training traffic tends not to have sufficient entropy [8]. Dynamic Load Balancing offers more responsive load balancing at a flow or even packet level [14, 26, 36]. However, load balancing does not by itself solve the problem of overall congestion in the network fabric; it merely distributes it evenly. We need better congestion management schemes. AI traffic can also be bursty which also limits the above solutions which for all practical purposes, rely on network feedback, which is suboptimal [1]. Our approach aims for maximum flexibility, which points us to the Communications layer software stack. Knowledge about the traffic patterns, and the ability to do effective flow control drives DQPLB design, which seamlessly complements.

CN: 传输（Transport）：AI 传输领域有两条研究路线：负载均衡和拥塞管理。它们是解决网络中拥塞、缓冲和延迟这一整体问题的互补方法。ECMP 的默认负载均衡方案假设流具有较大的熵。然而，AI 训练流量往往不具备足够的熵 [8]。动态负载均衡（Dynamic Load Balancing）能在流级甚至包级提供更灵敏的负载均衡 [14, 26, 36]。然而，负载均衡本身并不能解决网络架构中整体拥塞的问题，它只是将拥塞均匀地分摊开来。我们需要更优的拥塞管理方案。AI 流量还可能具有突发性，这也限制了上述方案——它们实际上都依赖网络反馈，而这并非最优 [1]。我们的方法旨在追求最大灵活性，这指引我们转向通信层软件栈。对流量模式的了解以及进行有效流控的能力推动了 DQPLB 的设计，它能够无缝地补充……（原文此段末尾因换页截断）

EN: Emulation: Simulators (e.g., ASTRA-Sim [35]) and training-stack substitutions (e.g., SimAI [34]) are valuable for what-if analysis, but they typically do not execute the unmodified production collective stack under a real OS/network environment. Our CPU-based emulation provides a low-cost, high-signal way to surface these scale-dominant control-plane failures early, before full-scale GPU rollouts. Our experience shows that investing in unmodified-stack emulation and repeatable fault injection is a necessary way for control-plane correctness and operational hardening, complementing performance-oriented simulation tools.

CN: 仿真（Emulation）：模拟器（如 ASTRA-Sim [35]）和训练栈替代方案（如 SimAI [34]）对于假设性（what-if）分析很有价值，但它们通常不会在真实的操作系统/网络环境下执行未经修改的生产级集合通信栈。我们基于 CPU 的仿真提供了一种低成本、高信号的方式，能够在全规模 GPU 部署之前，尽早暴露这些由规模主导的控制平面故障。我们的经验表明，投资于未修改栈的仿真以及可重复的故障注入，是保障控制平面正确性与运维加固的必要途径，与面向性能的模拟工具形成互补。

## 8 Conclusion（结论）

EN: The physical infrastructure of AI clusters is transitioning toward multi-gigawatt scale deployments, such as Colossus 2 [18], Rainier [19], Stargate [24], and Hyperion [32]. These next-generation facilities will house more than one million GPUs, shifting from multi-building to regional-scale interconnects. As we look toward these massive deployments, the communication stack must evolve to address the unique engineering hurdles that accompany this unprecedented scale.

CN: AI 集群的物理基础设施正在向多吉瓦（multi-gigawatt）规模的部署演进，例如 Colossus 2 [18]、Rainier [19]、Stargate [24] 和 Hyperion [32]。这些下一代设施将容纳超过一百万块 GPU，互连方式也从多栋建筑级转向区域级。当我们展望这些大规模部署时，通信栈必须随之演进，以应对这一空前规模所带来的独特工程挑战。

EN: At 100K+ scale, physical topology constraints and the bursty nature of workloads renders existing techniques insufficient. By co-designing a communication library with the transport layer and a suite of operational tools, we built a robust foundation that sustains high-goodput training of frontier LLMs despite scale, latency variance, and frequent faults. This experience provides a blueprint for the industry as LLM training moves toward larger deployments.

CN: 在 100K+ 规模下，物理拓扑约束与工作负载的突发性使现有技术捉襟见肘。通过将通信库与传输层以及一整套运维工具协同设计，我们构建了一个稳健的基础，即便面对规模、延迟波动和频繁故障，也能维持前沿 LLM 的高有效产出（high-goodput）训练。这一经验为业界在 LLM 训练迈向更大规模部署时提供了一份蓝图。

EN: This work does not raise any ethical issues.

CN: 本工作不涉及任何伦理问题。
